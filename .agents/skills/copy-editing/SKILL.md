# Copy Editing & Content Strategy

A framework for transforming generic text into high-impact, authentic, and clear communication.

## 1. The Seven Sweeps Framework
1. **Clarity**: Is it easy to understand? Remove jargon.
2. **Voice & Tone**: Does it sound authentic? Match the brand's unique soul (e.g., the "Choquero" persona).
3. **So What?**: Why should the reader care? Focus on benefits, not just features.
4. **Prove It**: Use data, testimonials, or specific examples to build trust.
5. **Specificity**: Avoid generic claims. Use concrete details (e.g., "78% faster" instead of "very fast").
6. **Heightened Emotion**: Use evocative language to create a connection.
7. **Zero Risk**: Address objections and build confidence.

## 2. Quick-Pass Editing Checks
- **Word-Level**: Kill adverbs. Swap weak verbs for strong ones.
- **Sentence-Level**: Vary sentence length. One idea per sentence.
- **Paragraph-Level**: Use short paragraphs. Ensure a logical flow and strong transitions.

## 3. Common Problems & Fixes
- **Wall of Features**: Flip features into benefits.
- **Corporate Speak**: Translate into human conversation.
- **Weak Opening**: Start with a hook or a bold statement.
- **Generic Claims**: Replace with unique, authentic anecdotes.

## 4. Local Soul Synthesis
- **Contextualize**: Connect global or external news to the local reader's daily life.
- **Add Flavor**: Use local idioms, place names, and cultural references to establish authority.
- **Directness**: Be honest and direct. Avoid marketing "fluff".
