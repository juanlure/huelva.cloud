# Content Research Writer

A framework for systematic research, collaborative outlining, and high-quality article production.

## 1. Research Workflow
- **Topic Deep-Dive**: Start with clarifying questions about the argument, audience, and goals.
- **Primary Sources**: Prioritize local, official, and authoritative sources for Huelva-specific content.
- **Fact Verification**: Cross-reference key data points before inclusion.

## 2. Collaborative Outlining
- **Hook Strategy**: Design a compelling opening that grabs attention.
- **Logical Flow**: Structure content with `H2` and `H3` subtitles to guide the reader.
- **Conclusion**: End with a call to action or a thought-provoking final insight.

## 3. Feedback & Refinement
- **The Seven Sweeps**: Apply the copy-editing framework (Clarity, Voice, So What?, Prove It, Specificity, Emotion, Zero Risk).
- **Style Alignment**: Ensure the "Choquero" soul is present in every paragraph.
- **Pre-Publish Checklist**:
    - [ ] Compelling hook
    - [ ] Logical transitions
    - [ ] Factually verified
    - [ ] Localized tone
    - [ ] SEO optimized

## 4. Writing Workflows
- **Blog Post**: Focus on narrative and engagement.
- **News**: Focus on synthesis and timeliness.
- **Guides**: Focus on utility and local expertise.
