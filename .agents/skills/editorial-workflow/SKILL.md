# Editorial Workflow Management

A framework for maintaining high standards in journalism and content production at Huelva.cloud.

## 1. Story Status Taxonomy
- **Drafting**: Content creation is in progress.
- **Reviewing**: Content is being checked for accuracy and tone.
- **Approved**: Content is ready for publication.
- **Published**: Content is live.

## 2. Assignment Handoff Checklist
- **Clear Objective**: What is the core story?
- **Source Verification**: Are there at least two reliable sources for critical facts?
- **Tone Alignment**: Does it match the assigned author's persona?
- **Multimedia**: Are high-quality, authentic images associated?

## 3. Publication Checklist (Pre-Publish)
- **Fact-Check**: Verify all names, dates, and locations.
- **Tone Sweep**: Ensure it sounds like a local ("Choquero" soul).
- **SEO Optimization**: Title tags, meta descriptions, and descriptive alt text for images.
- **Link Check**: Verify all external and internal links work.

## 4. Edit Handoff Protocols
- **Reporter → Editor**: Provide a summary of findings and any potential missing data or unverified claims.
- **Editor → Reporter**: Provide specific, actionable feedback on tone, clarity, and structural improvements.

## 5. Accuracy & Verification
- **Reference Goal**: Huelva.cloud aims to be a primary reference. Synthesize information into original narratives rather than just quoting external sources.
- **Transparency**: Use AI-atribution correctly for generated content.
- **Source Citation**: Always cite original sources in a discrete footer.
